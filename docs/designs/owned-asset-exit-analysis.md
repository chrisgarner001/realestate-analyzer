## Problem Statement

IRES holds a portfolio of vacant single-family homes in Michigan (the sample is Harper Woods), many returned through land-contract forfeiture (9 of 19 in the sample). Each sits costing property tax and insurance, and is exposed to theft (5 of 19 had furnaces or water heaters stolen) while staff decide what to do with it. The sample's 19 homes carry about **$86k/yr in property tax** and have accrued roughly **$133k in tax alone while vacant**. They need a per-property recommendation across four exits, run across 60+ properties at once, from data they already export.

## Status Quo

Staff maintain an inventory export per partnership/city with, per property: portfolio, address, status, move-out date, listing agent, list price, "goal", comps value, Zillow update date, listed date, sale status, rehab status (For Sale, For Sale As Is, Quote Sent, Quote Needed, Hold for Funds, Pending Sale), rehab quote amount, free-text rehab and mechanical notes, the two property tax bills, and deactivation notes (land contract history, loan removal). ## Exit models (all computed in Python, `web/owned_asset.py`)

Common: **monthly carry** = (annual_tax + annual_insurance)/12 + monthly_utilities_maint. **Cost of waiting** per property = monthly carry, shown in the summary. Loan payoff (if any) is subtracted when the property is sold or when a land contract closes (see Open Questions for wrap-around LCs).

1. **Sell as-is:** `as_is_value` (model-estimated from comps, or override; default fallback `arv − rehab_quote`) − selling costs − payoff − carry × `months_to_sell_as_is` (default 2). Output: net cash, month received.
2. **Rehab → sell retail:** `arv` − rehab_quote − pos_inspection_cost − selling costs − payoff − carry × (`rehab_months` 2 + `months_to_sell_retail` 3). Output: net cash, month received, cash needed up front (rehab).
3. **Re-sell on land contract:** sale price `lc_price` (default `arv`, optional rehab first), down payment % (10), rate (8%), term (30 yr): existing index.html land-contract defaults. Output: down payment received now, monthly payment, total collected over `hold_years` (5) plus balloon/payoff assumption at horizon, IRR with year 0 = as-is net proceeds (opportunity cost, carried from eng R1). **Forfeiture risk flag** when history_notes show a prior LC forfeiture on the property.
4. **Rehab → rent:** market rent from PropYield's rental comps (model estimate, override allowed); NOI with vacancy (5%), management (8%), capex reserve (5%), tax, insurance; rehab up front; `lease_up_months` (2); IRR over `hold_years` with exit at `arv` appreciated. Year 0 = as-is net proceeds + rehab (opportunity cost).

**Recommendation rule (deterministic ranking, model explains):** rank exits by value in today's dollars at a `discount_rate_pct` (default 10%) so cash now, cash at month 5, and a payment stream compare on one scale. The model writes the narrative and may flag reasons to override (e.g., theft risk, inspection failures), but it never changes the numbers.

## Open Questions (for re-review)

- **Batch execution under Vercel's 300 s limit (eng):** client-driven queue (browser sends one /analyze per property, 2-3 at a time) vs. a server-side job table polled by the browser. 60 properties × 2 model calls each ≈ tens of minutes in total.
- **Per-property narrative length and cost (eng):** full narrative per property vs. short 3-5 sentence rationale plus a full narrative on demand. At $25 revenue, per-report model and web-search cost must stay well under that; not yet measured.
- **Pending Sale rows:** skip, summarize, or analyze anyway? (4 of 19 are pending.)
- **Land contract with a portfolio loan:** is the loan paid off at LC closing, or wrapped? (Affects cash at closing; could be negative.)
- **Point-of-sale inspection costs:** Harper Woods and other Michigan cities require one before sale; is there a typical cost to default, or does IRES enter it?
- **Market rent source for vacant homes:** PropYield's rental comps (model + web search) with an override; confirm IRES has no rent targets.
- **Discount rate** IRES uses to compare cash now vs. later (default 10%).
- **CSV variations:** the sample is one partnership/city; confirm other exports share the same columns.

## Exit engine spec adoption (S1, founder decision 2026-09-24)

Source: the founder's "Exit Strategy Analyzer: AI Agent System Prompt (v1)", saved as **docs/specs/exit-strategy-analyzer-v1.md** (renamed PropMind.AI → PropYield). Founder decision: use it as the **spec for the Python engine**, not as a prompt that does math.

- `web/owned_asset.py` implements the spec's Defaults table, Pre-flight checks, Scenario models A-D, Comparison metrics, the 7 Stress tests, Break-even triggers and Decision rules, and emits the spec's `analysis_json` exactly.
- The model does only two things: (1) DATA pulls (as-is comps, ARV comps, market rent, DOM as-is/renovated, appreciation, months of supply / price cuts), each tagged DATA with source; (2) `report_markdown` written from the computed `analysis_json`, following the spec's Output Format and Guardrails (no process narration, point estimates, disclaimer line).
- Horizon 36 months (spec default), discount rate 8%, itemized selling costs (5.5% + 1.5% + 0.86% MI transfer tax + concessions), and every other value in the spec's Defaults table replace this doc's earlier defaults.
- **Land-contract compliance (founder, 2026-09-24):** IRES's land contracts are serviced by SGMS, so compliance is handled. New per-tenant setting `lc_servicer` (e.g. "SGMS"): when set, the spec's "Compliance Review Required" flag is downgraded to an info flag "LC servicing and compliance: {lc_servicer}". Tenants without it keep the warning. The disclaimer line stays for everyone. Servicing cost keeps the spec default ($25/mo), editable per batch.

**S1 clarifications (founder, 2026-09-24):**
- **S1a Risk Score:** `1 + 9 × clamp((base_npv − min(stress_npvs)) ÷ |base_npv|, 0, 1)`, rounded to an integer, +1 per critical risk flag (e.g., LC forfeiture history, rehab + contingency > 30% of ARV), capped at 10. If base_npv = 0, spread = 1. Unit tests: no downside → 1; worst case loses all of base NPV → 10; flag increments and cap.
- **S1b Missing property facts:** beds/baths/sqft/year built are pulled by the estimate call from public records (tagged DATA with source). If not found, the row still runs with those facts tagged DEFAULT, confidence capped at Low, and a data flag in the summary. The spec's pre-1960 contingency rule (20%) applies only when year built is known; unknown year built uses 20% ("scope unverified"), which is the spec's conservative branch.

Question D1:

Project/branch/task: S1 engine math review on main.
ELI10: Your spec compares every strategy "from the owner's current equity today." There are two ways to code that.
